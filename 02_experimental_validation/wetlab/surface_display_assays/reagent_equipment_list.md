# Reagent and Equipment List for LiSPER Whole-Cell Li+/Na+ Assays

This list is written for a practical DKU/Kunshan wet-lab workflow. Specific supplier choice should follow local purchasing rules, but common China-accessible suppliers include Sangon Biotech, Macklin, Aladdin, Beyotime, Solarbio, Yeasen, Thermo Fisher China, Merck/Sigma China, and local analytical testing providers.

## Chemicals and Standards

| Item | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| LiCl, high purity | Li+ binding solutions | Use high-purity salt; record lot and purity. |
| NaCl, high purity | Na+ competition solutions | Add only deliberately; avoid sodium in buffers. |
| Certified Li standard solution | ICP calibration/QC | Prefer commercial standard over homemade calibration for validation. |
| Certified Na standard solution | ICP calibration/QC | Matrix-match standards where possible. |
| KCl, high purity | Osmotic support | Preferred over NaCl for assay buffer background. |
| HEPES free acid | Low-sodium assay buffer | Adjust pH with KOH, not NaOH. |
| PIPES free acid | Alternative low-sodium buffer | Useful pH range near neutral. |
| KOH, high purity | Buffer pH adjustment | Avoid NaOH. |
| Trace-metal-grade HNO3 | ICP preservation/digestion | Follow analytical facility requirements. |
| Ultrapure water | Buffer/standard prep | Use high-resistivity water. |
| NiCl2 or NiSO4 | Residual metal competition | Use low mg/L ranges first. |
| CoCl2 or CoSO4 | Residual metal competition | Toxic; handle per institutional SOP. |
| MnCl2 or MnSO4 | Residual metal competition | Include after simple Li/Na assays work. |
| FeCl3 | Residual metal competition | Hydrolysis/precipitation risk; prepare fresh and control pH. |
| AlCl3 | Residual metal competition | Hydrolysis/precipitation risk. |
| CuCl2 or CuSO4 | Residual metal competition | Strong peptide/cell-envelope binder; use carefully. |

Avoid:

- EDTA, citrate, and strong chelators in binding assays.
- Sodium phosphate, sodium carbonate, sodium hydroxide, sodium azide, and sodium-containing wash buffers unless deliberately tested.
- Glassware or plastics with unknown Li/Na contamination for quantitative work.

## Outsourcing Notes

For DKU/Kunshan practicality:

- Batch samples to reduce per-sample ICP cost.
- Ask whether the facility prefers HNO3 concentration, sample volume, filtration, and acceptable salt load.
- Provide expected concentration ranges for Li and Na.
- Include blanks, standards, and duplicate samples in every shipment.
- For high-Na samples, warn the facility about matrix load and request dilution guidance.
