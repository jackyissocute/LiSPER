# 05 Cell Harvest, Lysis, and Clarification Protocol

## Purpose

Collect induced biomass and prepare clarified soluble lysate for Ni-NTA purification.

## Inputs

- Optimized induced culture.
- Lysis buffer compatible with His-tag purification.
- Protease inhibitor if available.
- Sonicator, lysozyme workflow, or other lab-approved lysis method.
- Refrigerated centrifuge.

## Step-by-Step Workflow

1. Record culture volume and final OD600.
2. Pellet cells by centrifugation.
3. Remove supernatant completely.
4. Record pellet wet mass.
5. Resuspend pellet in cold lysis buffer.
6. Lyse cells using an approved method.
7. Keep samples cold during lysis.
8. Clarify lysate by centrifugation.
9. Save aliquots of total lysate, soluble lysate, and pellet for gel analysis.
10. Proceed to Ni-NTA purification or freeze clarified lysate only if validated.

## Expected Outcome

- Clear or slightly turbid soluble lysate containing His6-SUMO-LiSPER.

## Common Failure Modes

| Problem | Likely cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Viscous lysate | genomic DNA release | Increase clarification, use nuclease if lab-approved. |
| Fusion in insoluble pellet | poor solubility | Revisit expression temperature/IPTG. |
| Protein degradation | proteases or warm handling | Work cold and shorten handling time. |
| Low protein in lysate | low biomass or poor lysis | Optimize lysis method and resuspension volume. |

## Records To Keep

- Culture volume.
- OD600.
- Pellet mass.
- Lysis buffer.
- Lysis method and time.
- Clarification conditions.
