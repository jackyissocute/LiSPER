# Surface-Display Host Selection Literature Search Strategy

Date searched: 2026-06-14

Review type: scoping technology assessment for LiSPER bacterial surface-display deployment. The goal was evidence-supported host and display-platform selection, not novelty seeking.

## Research Questions

1. What is the realistic industrial environment at the lithium polishing/recovery stage after spent-LIB acid leaching and Cu/Al/Fe/Co/Mn/Ni removal?
2. What temperatures, pH ranges, residence times, and sequence positions are common for lithium capture and lithium carbonate production?
3. Which microbial hosts could realistically operate in that environment or in an engineered sidestream derived from it?
4. Which bacterial display systems best support small LiSPER peptides of approximately 1-2 kDa?

## Inclusion Criteria

- Peer-reviewed papers, open technical reports, reviews, theses, and reputable industry reports.
- Sources describing spent lithium-ion battery hydrometallurgy, lithium carbonate precipitation, direct lithium extraction, lithium ion-sieve/adsorbent operation, biosorption, microbial chassis robustness, and bacterial surface display.
- Sources with quantitative operating information were prioritized over broad narrative reviews.

## Exclusion Criteria

- Raw battery-leaching papers with no lithium recovery/polishing relevance.
- Host papers that only demonstrate intracellular expression with no surface-display or immobilization relevance.
- Display systems used only for large enzymes where no peptide-display precedent exists.
