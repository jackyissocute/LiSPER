Date searched: 2026-06-14

## Scope

This study evaluates realistic industrial deployment formats for LiSPER after peptide validation, including living cells, inactivated display cells, purified immobilized peptide, membrane fragments, magnetic beads, packed-bed columns, and hybrid systems.

## Selection Criteria

Included sources had to inform at least one of:

- practical deployment architecture,
- immobilization chemistry,
- support material choice,
- column operation/regeneration,
- lithium capture or carbonate conversion,
- biosorption scale-up risk,
- Li-specific peptide immobilization precedent.
