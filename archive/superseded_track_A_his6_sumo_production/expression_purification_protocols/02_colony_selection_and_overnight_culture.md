# 02 Colony Selection and Overnight Culture Protocol

## Purpose

Select individual transformants, establish confirmed working cultures, and prepare overnight cultures for expression testing.

## Why This Matters

Expression failures are hard to interpret if clone identity is uncertain. Colony confirmation and careful culture records prevent wasted purification attempts.

## Inputs

- Fresh transformation plate.
- LB-kanamycin medium.
- Sterile culture tubes or deep-well plate.
- Colony PCR reagents or diagnostic confirmation method.
- Glycerol stock materials.

## Step-by-Step Workflow

1. Pick 2-3 well-isolated colonies per construct.
2. Streak each colony onto a labeled backup plate or patch area.
3. Inoculate each colony into small LB-kanamycin overnight culture.
4. Incubate overnight with shaking.
5. Use a small aliquot for colony PCR or diagnostic verification.
6. Keep cultures at 4 C temporarily while waiting for confirmation.
7. Make glycerol stocks from confirmed clones.
8. Choose one confirmed clone per construct for expression testing.

## Confirmation Options

| Method | Use |
|---|---|
| Colony PCR | Fast insert-size confirmation. |
| Plasmid miniprep + diagnostic digest | Confirms plasmid size/pattern. |
| Sanger sequencing | Best for junction/insert confirmation if needed. |
| Vendor full sequence record | Useful but should still be paired with local clone identity check. |

## Expected Outcome

- At least one confirmed clone per construct.
- Backup plate and glycerol stock for each confirmed clone.

## Common Failure Modes

| Problem | Likely cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Mixed colony | colony picked from crowded region | Re-streak and isolate again. |
| PCR weak or absent | primer/template issue | Use lysed colony template, adjust annealing, or miniprep. |
| Wrong band | wrong colony or plasmid rearrangement | Test another colony and confirm with sequencing. |
| No overnight growth | wrong antibiotic or stressed clone | Verify antibiotic, incubate longer, use fresh colony. |

## Records To Keep

- Colony IDs.
- Culture IDs.
- PCR/digest result.
- Glycerol stock location.
- Selected clone for expression.
