# 01 Transformation Protocol

## Purpose

Introduce vendor-delivered His6-SUMO-LiSPER plasmids into an expression-compatible E. coli strain, most likely BL21(DE3) or a similar T7 expression host.

## Scientific Rationale

Transformation creates the expression strain needed for Track A. This step does not test Li binding; it only establishes verified clone material for expression.

## Inputs

- Vendor-delivered His6-SUMO-LiSPER plasmid DNA.
- Chemically competent or electrocompetent BL21(DE3)-compatible cells.
- LB or SOC recovery medium.
- LB-kanamycin plates.
- Positive transformation control plasmid.
- No-DNA negative control.

## Recommended Starting Conditions

| Parameter | Starting value |
|---|---|
| Host | BL21(DE3) or equivalent T7 expression strain |
| Antibiotic | Kanamycin, concentration per lab standard and vector requirement |
| Recovery | SOC or LB, 45-60 min |
| Plate incubation | 30-37 C overnight |

## Step-by-Step Workflow

1. Label all tubes and plates before thawing cells.
2. Thaw competent cells on ice.
3. Add plasmid DNA gently to competent cells.
4. Include positive-control and no-DNA control transformations.
5. Incubate on ice according to competent-cell protocol.
6. Heat shock or electroporate according to cell type.
7. Add recovery medium.
8. Recover with shaking.
9. Plate appropriate volumes on LB-kanamycin plates.
10. Incubate overnight.

## Expected Outcome

- Experimental plates show colonies.
- Positive control shows colonies.
- No-DNA control shows no colonies.

## Common Failure Modes

| Problem | Likely cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| No colonies anywhere | incompetent cells or failed recovery | Repeat with fresh competent cells and positive control. |
| Colonies on no-DNA control | antibiotic failure or contamination | Make fresh plates and repeat. |
| Very tiny colonies | expression leakiness or stress | Incubate at 30 C; consider glucose in recovery/plates if standard for host. |
| Too many colonies to isolate | plated too much recovery | Re-streak single colonies. |

## Records To Keep

- Plasmid ID.
- Host strain.
- Competent-cell lot.
- Antibiotic concentration.
- Colony count estimate.
- Plate photos if possible.
