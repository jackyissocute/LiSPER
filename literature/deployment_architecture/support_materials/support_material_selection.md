# Support Material Selection

## Support Material Ranking

| Rank | Support | Recommended role | Rationale |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Epoxy acrylic/methacrylate resin beads | Industrial packed-bed LiSPER peptide adsorbent | Strong covalent chemistry, good mechanical properties, enzyme-immobilization precedent, reusable columns. |
| 2 | NHS/maleimide agarose beads | Lab and early pilot validation | Easy bioconjugation, familiar affinity-media handling, gentle chemistry. |
| 3 | Magnetic beads | Screening, process analytics, small modular capture | Easy separation, Li-binding peptide precedent, high cost for bulk deployment. |
| 4 | Silica particles/monoliths | Rigid columns or membranes under moderate pH | Cheap and mechanically strong; vulnerable to high pH. |
| 5 | Inactivated display-cell biomass in alginate/polymer beads | Low-cost bridge architecture | Retains surface display without live GMO operation; mass transfer and stability need testing. |
| 6 | Membrane fragments / outer-membrane vesicles | High-density display research | Less regulatory burden than live cells but harder to manufacture consistently. |
| 7 | Living immobilized bacteria | Research-only or niche self-regenerating beds | Lowest material cost but highest regulatory/containment and operational risk. |

## Recommended Support Material

The preferred post-validation support is an epoxy-functionalized acrylic or methacrylate resin bead with terminally immobilized LiSPER peptide through a designed spacer.

Design requirements:

- bead diameter suitable for packed-bed pressure drop and mass transfer,
- low nonspecific Li/Na adsorption background,
- chemical stability at pH 5-9 and during regeneration,
- minimal swelling/shrinkage across salt gradients,
- compatible with clean-in-place or mild sanitization,
- low ligand leakage,
- terminal peptide orientation preserving the Li-binding motif.

## Why Not Living Cells as the Main Support?

Living cells are attractive for low-cost self-production, but they introduce:

- GMO containment and release risk,
- unpredictable viability under high ionic strength and residual metals,
- metabolic drift,
- biofouling,
- endotoxin/biomass handling concerns for Gram-negative hosts,
- difficult validation of consistent ligand density.

Living display remains useful for discovery and perhaps regenerable bioreactors, but it is a poor first industrial architecture.

## Why Not Magnetic Beads as the Main Support?

Magnetic beads are excellent for proof-of-concept because separation is simple and the Li-binding peptide literature now includes magnetic bead precedent. However, bead cost, magnetic separator scale, attrition, and solids handling make them less attractive than packed resin for bulk lithium-polishing streams.
