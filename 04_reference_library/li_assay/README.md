# Li+ affinity, selectivity, and removal assay sources

Search completed 2026-08-09. Sources were collected without restricting publication date from PubMed, Europe PMC, Crossref, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, publisher sites, and general web search. Query families covered lithium-binding peptides, lithium adsorption/recovery, Li+/Na+ selectivity, ICP-OES, flame photometry, colorimetry, ITC, dialysis, and 7Li NMR.

## Direct peptide–Li+ evidence

| Source | Raw local material | What was experimentally tested | Important limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bhargawa, Hong & Yoo, *Desalination* 577 (2024) 117412. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.desal.2024.117412) | `Bhargawa_2024_Desalination_magnetic_LBP_beads_supplement.docx`; `..._metadata.json` | EDC/NHS-coupled lithium-binding peptides on magnetic beads; adsorption, desorption/reuse, capacity, and competing metals. This is the closest published bead-assay precedent. | The reported competition panel does not establish Li+/Na+ selectivity; non-terminal amines in a new sequence could make EDC/NHS attachment heterogeneous. Main article is paywalled, so the official supplement and metadata are stored. |
| Selvamani et al., *J. Environ. Chem. Eng.* 11 (2023) 109029. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jece.2022.109029) | `Selvamani_2023_JECE_surface_display_LBP_supplement.docx`; `..._metadata.json` | GPGAP repeat peptides displayed on *E. coli* for Li+ removal and competing-metal tests. | A whole-cell display system, not isolated peptide beads; Na+ competition was not demonstrated. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/jimb/kuae012) | `Jeong_2024_JIMB_industrial_waste_LBP_surface_display.pdf`; `...xml` | Lithium-binding peptide surface display applied to an industrial battery-waste solution. | Whole-cell format and a complex waste matrix; does not by itself validate bead-bound nominated sequences. |

## Screening boundaries

Included peer-reviewed experiments with direct Li+-peptide evidence or a clearly transferable Li+ measurement method. Excluded computational-only predictions, phage binding to solid lithium-battery materials rather than free aqueous Li+, vendor-only protocols, and untested claims. A visible signal is not automatically an affinity constant: adsorption capacity/removal, equilibrium affinity, and selectivity against Na+/K+/Mg2+/Ca2+ need separate controls and mass-balance measurements.
